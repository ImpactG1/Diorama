## World & Era

Prohibition-era America, 1925-1933. Speakeasies hidden behind barbershops and jazz clubs. Tommy guns, fedora silhouettes, brass fixtures, cigarette smoke, Art Deco geometry. NOT generic "gangster" clip-art — research real Prohibition-era details: peeling paint on brick walls, ornate but worn brass light fixtures, leather club chairs, bootleg liquor labels, Ford Model T silhouettes.

## Lighting Rule

> **Warm tungsten key light from screen-left, casting long dramatic shadows to the right. Deep vignette darkening all four edges. Fine 35mm film grain visible on close inspection.**

This exact sentence is appended to every asset prompt.

## Render Medium

> **Painterly digital illustration with visible brush strokes, reminiscent of noir movie posters from the 1940s. Rich, saturated shadows with slightly exaggerated contrast. Not photorealistic, not flat vector — painterly.**

## Motion Personality

**Slow, heavy, deliberate.** Like smoke settling in a still room. Nothing snappy, nothing bouncy. Easing: `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.
